All available treatments

Preventive

General Check-Up

For Anyone who hasn't been in a while, or wants to stay ahead of problems.

A thorough look at your teeth, gums, and jaw — using digital X-rays if needed. We flag anything that's developing before it becomes a bigger issue.

Outcome You leave knowing exactly what's happening in your mouth and what, if anything, needs attention.

Preventive

Teeth Cleaning

For People who brush regularly but still notice build-up, sensitivity, or dullness.

We remove tartar and plaque that brushing can't reach — especially along the gum line and between teeth. It's not painful, and most people find it surprisingly satisfying.

Outcome Cleaner, smoother teeth — and gums that are less likely to bleed or become inflamed.

Book this treatment
Restorative

Fillings

For Anyone with a cavity, a chipped tooth, or an old metal filling they'd like replaced.

We clean out the decayed area and seal the tooth with a tooth-coloured resin. The process is straightforward — most fillings are done in a single visit.

Outcome The tooth is sealed and protected. No visible metal. Biting and chewing return to normal.

Book this treatment
Restorative

Tooth Extraction

For Teeth that are too damaged to save, severely infected, or impacted wisdom teeth.

We numb the area fully before anything happens. The extraction itself is quick — most of the time it's the build-up beforehand that feels worse than the procedure.

Outcome The source of pain or infection is removed. We'll discuss replacement options before you leave.

Book this treatment
Restorative

Root Canal

For A tooth with deep infection, persistent ache, or one that's sensitive to temperature long after eating.

We remove the infected pulp inside the tooth, clean the root canal, and seal it. It has a reputation for being painful — the reality is it relieves pain, not causes it.

Outcome The tooth is saved. Infection is cleared. Most patients say they wish they'd come sooner.

Book this treatment
Restorative

Crowns

For Teeth that are cracked, severely worn down, or weakened after a root canal or large filling.

A crown is a cap that fits over the entire visible tooth. It's shaped and coloured to blend in, so it looks like a normal tooth — because it effectively becomes one.

Outcome A tooth that can chew, look, and function like it never had a problem.

Book this treatment
Restorative

Bridges

For Anyone missing one or more teeth with healthy teeth on either side to anchor to.

A bridge spans the gap left by a missing tooth, anchored to the neighbouring teeth. It's fixed — not removable — and feels close to having your natural tooth back.

Outcome The gap is closed. Speech, chewing, and appearance return to normal.

Book this treatment
Restorative

Dental Implants

For People missing one or more teeth who want a permanent solution that looks and feels natural.

A titanium post is placed into the jawbone, acting as a root. A crown is then attached on top. The result is a tooth that's fixed in place, not removable — no slipping, no paste.

Outcome A permanent tooth replacement that protects your jawbone and doesn't affect neighbouring teeth.

Book this treatment
Restorative

Dentures

For Those who have lost most or all of their teeth and want a comfortable, practical solution.

Custom-fitted removable teeth — full or partial — designed to fit your mouth specifically. Modern dentures are lighter and more natural-looking than older versions.

Outcome The ability to eat, speak, and smile without self-consciousness. A complete set of teeth again.

Book this treatment
Cosmetic

Teeth Whitening

For Anyone bothered by staining from coffee, tea, food, or just general yellowing over time.

A professional-grade bleaching treatment applied under clinical conditions — far more effective than over-the-counter products and safer than DIY kits that can damage enamel.

Outcome Noticeably brighter teeth — typically several shades lighter — in a single session.

Book this treatment
Cosmetic

Veneers

For People who want to change the shape, size, or colour of their front teeth long-term.

Thin porcelain shells bonded to the front surface of your teeth. We'll show you what the result will look like before anything is permanent — no surprises.

Outcome A consistent, natural-looking smile that doesn't require constant upkeep beyond normal brushing.

Book this treatment
Cosmetic

Orthodontics

For Children, teens, or adults with crowded, crooked, or unevenly spaced teeth.

Braces or clear aligners that gradually move teeth into better alignment. We map the full treatment plan upfront so you know the timeline before you start.

Outcome Straighter teeth that are easier to clean and a bite that works the way it should.
